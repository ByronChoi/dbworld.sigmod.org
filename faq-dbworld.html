Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about subscriptions, posting accounts, and submitting messages to DBWorld.

1.I subscribed to DBWorld, but received “invalid email/password combination” when trying to post a message. Why?

A subscription account is only for receiving DBWorld messages; it cannot be used for posting. To post a message, you need to register for a posting account.

If the problem remains, send the moderators at dbworldmoderator@gmail.com your message and the information needed to complete the posting form. The moderators may post the message on your behalf.
