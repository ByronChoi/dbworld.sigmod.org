Frequently Asked Questions

1. I have subscribed to DBWorld, but got the error message "invalid email/password combination" when
    trying to post a message. Why?

A subscription account is only for receiving DBWorld messages; it cannot be used for posting. To post a message, you
need to register for a posting account here.


If the problem remains, please send us (dbworldmoderator@gmail.com) your message and the information to fill in the posting form.
We will post the message for you.
