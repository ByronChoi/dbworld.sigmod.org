Post a Message to DBWorld

Choose an announcement category below. To post, you must register with DBWorld and use the posting password issued for your account.

All messages should be directly related to database research. Please do not post person-specific information such as a resume. If you do, you may be permanently banned from posting to DBWorld.

The categories below cover the appropriate message types. Please do not use the News category as a generic catch-all. Changes of address, surveys, “looking for jobs/people/papers/etc.” messages, and requests for information should not be posted to DBWorld.

Please do not post multiple versions of the same announcement simultaneously. Plain ASCII is preferred for most email recipients.

Your message will be moderated and will typically appear on the list within 24 hours.

Posting limits: Each user may submit a maximum of 2 messages in any 24-hour period and 3 messages in any 7-day period. Violating these limits may result in loss of posting privileges. Creating different email accounts to circumvent the limits may result in a permanent ban. Please cooperate to keep traffic under control.
