SIGMOD/DBWorld

Welcome to DBWorld

A community service for announcements, calls for papers, positions, conferences, and other matters of interest to the database research community.

You can now read and post messages to DBWorld through the Web. Anyone can browse messages, but you must register to obtain a DBWorld posting password before you can post messages through the Web.

Posted messages will be moderated and are typically posted within 24 hours. Posting messages that are not appropriate to this list can lead to loss of posting privileges, so please avoid posting irrelevant messages.

Once you register, you can also change your password.

If you cannot post through the Web interface for any reason, contact the moderators at dbworldmoderator@gmail.com.

Receiving messages by email

To receive messages by email, you must subscribe to the DBWorld mailing list. Note that registering to post messages does not automatically subscribe you to the mailing list.

To subscribe, send mail to listserv@listserv.acm.org with one of the following lines in the message body:

// SUBSCRIBE mod-dbworld your-name
// SUBSCRIBE mod-dbworld your-name WITH digest

Use the first line for normal delivery or the digest form to receive queued posts in one larger message. Remember to include // at the start of the line and replace your-name with your name. The subject line of the message does not matter.

You will be enrolled in the mailing list after replying to the confirmation email.

To unsubscribe, send the following in the message body:

// UNSUBSCRIBE mod-dbworld

Please do not send subscribe or unsubscribe commands to dbworldmoderator@gmail.com. That address is not connected to the subscription process.

Contact us at dbworldmoderator@gmail.com if you have problems. Being on this list means you receive posted messages by email, but does not authorize you to post messages through this website.
