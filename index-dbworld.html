 You can now read and post messages to DBWorld through the Web. Anyone can browse messages, but you must register to obtain a DBWorld posting password before you can post messages through the Web. Posted messages will be moderated and are typically posted within 24 hours. (Posting messages that are not appropriate to this list can lead to loss of posting privileges, so please avoid posting irrelevant messages.) Once you register, you can also change your password.

If you can't post through the Web interface for any reason, contact the moderators dbworldmoderator@gmail.com.

RECEIVING MSGS BY EMAIL: To receive msgs by email, you must subscribe to the dbworld mailing list. (Note that registering to post msgs does NOT automatically subscribe you to the mailing list!) To subscribe, send mail to listserv@listserv.acm.org with one of the following lines in your message body: 

	  // SUBSCRIBE mod-dbworld your-name
	  // SUBSCRIBE mod-dbworld your-name WITH digest
depending on whether you want the message to be delivered in digest mode or in the normal mode. (In nodigest mode, all the posts will be sent to you directly. In digest mode, the posts will be held for a while and then sent to you in a large message containing all the queued-up posts.) Remember to include the // at the start of the line, and replace your-name with your name. The subject line of your message does not matter and can be anything.

You will be enrolled in the mailing list after replying to the confirmation email.

To unsubscribe, the msg body should contain 

	  // UNSUBSCRIBE mod-dbworld

Please do not send subscribe or unsubscribe commands to dbworldmoderator@gmail.com. It is not connected to the subscribe/unsubscribe process.

Contact us (dbworldmoderator@gmail.com) if you have problems. (Being on this list means you receive posted messages by email, but does not authorize you to post messages via this Web page.)
